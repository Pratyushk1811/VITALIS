I want to completely replace the dotted particle / atom aesthetic.

The current particle approach feels generic, AI-generated, and too close to atoms, stars, quantum visualizations, and futuristic SaaS websites.

DO NOT USE PARTICLES AS THE PRIMARY VISUAL LANGUAGE.

Instead, design VITALIS around a single continuous ABSTRACT 3D MATERIAL.

CORE IDEA:

A living computational material continuously changes form as the user scrolls.

Think:
- translucent fluid
- flowing volumetric material
- liquid surface
- sculptural smoke
- deformable mathematical surface
- soft 3D topology
- scientific visualization

NOT:
- dots
- stars
- atoms
- electrons
- DNA
- brains
- galaxies
- holograms
- neural-network diagrams

The material should feel like DATA has physical form.

--------------------------------------------------

SCROLL STORY

SECTION 01 — RAW DATA

Start with a large abstract 3D volume floating in a nearly black environment.

It should look like an unknown physical material.

It slowly deforms.

There is almost no UI around it.

Large typography:

VITALIS

HYBRID INTELLIGENCE
FOR EARLY DISEASE SCREENING

Supporting text:

Where biomedical data meets classical and quantum computation.

The 3D object should dominate the viewport.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 02 — FORM

As the user scrolls, the 3D material begins stretching and reorganizing.

It gradually forms a recognizable HUMAN SILHOUETTE.

The transformation must be continuous.

Do not crossfade between two images.

The same 3D material changes shape.

Text:

EVERY PATIENT
IS MORE THAN A DATASET.

Large typography.

Secondary text should remain smaller.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 03 — BIOLOGY

Continue scrolling.

The human silhouette deforms.

The torso region becomes an abstract cardiovascular structure.

The transformation should look like:

human form
→ deformation
→ flowing biological structure

Do not use a stock anatomical photograph.

Use an elegant scientific 3D form.

Text:

CARDIOVASCULAR

6 FEATURES
5 MODELS

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 04 — TRANSFORMATION

The biological structure begins to dissolve into flowing surfaces.

Those surfaces fold, stretch and reorganize.

The visual should communicate:

BIOLOGY
→
DATA
→
COMPUTATION

Large statement:

COMPLEXITY
BECOMES STRUCTURE.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 05 — COMPUTATION

The flowing material separates into two computational pathways.

CLASSICAL

Logistic Regression
Random Forest
RBF SVM

QUANTUM

QSVM
VQC

Represent the two approaches through DIFFERENT GEOMETRIC BEHAVIOURS rather than glowing cards.

For example:

Classical:
structured geometric surfaces

Quantum:
more complex multidimensional-looking deformation

But do NOT imply quantum superiority.

Text:

TWO COMPUTATIONAL PARADIGMS.

ONE EVALUATION FRAMEWORK.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 06 — CONVERGENCE

The two computational structures begin interacting.

They merge into a single structure.

The camera moves through the structure.

Reveal:

PREDICTION
CONSENSUS
EXPLAINABILITY
BENCHMARKING

Text:

DIFFERENT MODELS.
ONE SCREENING WORKFLOW.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 07 — BENCHMARK

The 3D structure transforms into a sophisticated scientific visualization.

The surface becomes:

- lines
- curves
- axes
- data points
- benchmark geometry

Show:

ACCURACY
PRECISION
SENSITIVITY
SPECIFICITY
F1
AUC

Make this look like a scientific research figure.

Not a SaaS dashboard.

Do not show:
BEST MODEL
WINNER
QUANTUM ADVANTAGE

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 08 — EXPLAINABILITY

The computational structure becomes transparent.

Individual regions of the geometry illuminate.

Show:

FEATURE
MODEL INFLUENCE
LOCAL SENSITIVITY

Large statement:

PREDICTION
SHOULD BE INSPECTABLE.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 09 — RESEARCH

Camera pulls far backward.

The entire computational environment becomes visible.

Display:

RESEARCH QUESTION

Can quantum-kernel and variational quantum approaches provide competitive performance against established classical ML models under identical evaluation conditions?

Technical metadata:

DATASET
FEATURE ENGINEERING
MODEL ARCHITECTURE
EVALUATION
REPRODUCIBILITY

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 10 — END

The entire 3D material slowly contracts.

Everything disappears into darkness.

Only:

VITALIS

remains.

Then:

EXPLORE THE INTELLIGENCE
BEHIND THE PREDICTION.

ENTER SCREENING

--------------------------------------------------

TYPOGRAPHY

DO NOT MAKE THE TEXT SMALL.

Use a strong editorial hierarchy.

Hero headline:
64–96px desktop

Major section headlines:
48–72px

Supporting text:
18–24px

Technical labels:
11–14px

Navigation:
13–15px

Buttons:
14–16px

Benchmark numbers:
36–64px

Use large typography with large negative space.

The goal is NOT tiny minimal text.

The goal is:

LARGE TYPE
+
LARGE 3D FORMS
+
LARGE EMPTY SPACE

Avoid putting text inside generic cards.

--------------------------------------------------

LAYOUT

Use asymmetry.

Do not center everything.

The 3D material should occupy 60–80% of the visual attention.

Text can sit:
- left edge
- right edge
- lower corner
- partially overlapping the 3D scene

Use sticky full-screen visual sections.

Scrolling should feel like controlling a scientific simulation.

--------------------------------------------------

COLOR

Near-black / graphite background.

Mostly off-white typography.

Very restrained mint.

Very restrained violet.

Color should primarily exist inside the 3D material.

Do not turn the whole interface purple.

--------------------------------------------------

OVERALL FEEL

The final website should feel like:

an experimental scientific visualization
+
a cinematic product launch
+
a research laboratory
+
an interactive 3D installation

It should NOT feel like:

an AI startup template
a quantum computing template
a medical SaaS dashboard
a cyberpunk website
a generic WebGL demo

The most important design principle:

THE 3D MATERIAL IS THE INTERFACE.

The user should scroll through one continuously transforming object rather than scroll through a collection of cards and animations.
